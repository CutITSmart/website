Lawful Bases and Data Protection Rights

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for collecting and using your personal information.

You have the following rights:

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to withdraw consent

If you make a request, we must respond without undue delay and within one month.

How Long We Keep Information

  • Customer records, correspondence, meeting notes, project records and quotations: up to 3 years after the business relationship ends or last meaningful contact.
